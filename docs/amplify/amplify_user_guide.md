# Amplify user guide

For every WSO2 employee. Amplify has one screen for you: **Celebrate**. This guide covers all of it.

Amplify lets you share WSO2's official LinkedIn posts with your own network in a couple of clicks, earn points for it, and see where you stand.

## Where to find it

Open **One WSO2**, switch to the **Amplify** perspective, and pick **Celebrate** in the left menu. That's the landing page.

(You may see other items in the menu, like New Post or Dashboarding. Those are for Marketing, PO and leadership, and they only appear for people with access. You don't need them.)

## What's on the page

Top to bottom:

1. **Posts to amplify**
2. **Top three amplifiers this month**, with the monthly rule and **My position**
3. **Team leaderboard**

### 1. Posts to amplify

Each card is one official WSO2 LinkedIn post: headline, date, image, a short summary, and chips.

- **Priority** chip: Marketing wants this one shared first. Priority posts sort to the top, then newest first.
- **Category** chip: Product, Company news, Customer story, Event, Thought leadership or Careers.
- Click the headline to read the original post on LinkedIn.

Three cards show at a time. If there are more, use the arrows and the "1–3 of 7" counter at the top right of the panel.

Once you've shared a post, it drops off your list. Share them all and the panel disappears. That means you're all caught up.

### 2. How to amplify a post

1. Click **Amplify** on a card.
2. **First time only:** you'll be asked to link your LinkedIn account (see below).
3. A dialog opens with the post. Optionally type your own thoughts in **Add your thoughts**. Up to 3,000 characters. They show above the repost on your LinkedIn.
4. Click **Amplify**.

You'll see "Queued. It goes out on your LinkedIn with the next Amplify run." Amplify checks for queued shares every few minutes, so it usually lands quickly but not instantly. You don't need to stay on the page.

If a share fails, the post stays in your list. Click **Amplify** again to retry it.

Points: a plain repost earns **2 points**. A repost with your own thoughts added earns **5 points**. You earn once per post.

### Linking your LinkedIn account

The first Amplify click opens **Link Your LinkedIn Account**. You have two choices.

**I consent.** You go to LinkedIn, approve the connection, and come straight back to Celebrate with the share dialog for that post already open. Amplify can then post reposts for you. It also lets WSO2 track the reach and engagement of what you share. The pop-up links to the WSO2 Employee Privacy Notice if you want the details.

**I don't consent.** That's a fine choice. Amplify won't connect to your account. You share the post yourself from your own LinkedIn, and you can give Amplify your profile URL (optional, like `https://www.linkedin.com/in/your-name`) so WSO2 can count your sharing. Click **Next** and you're back on Celebrate. The pop-up will come back the next time you click Amplify.

Your link lasts a while and then needs renewing. When it expires, the pop-up reads **Link Your LinkedIn Account Again**. Just consent again.

**To check or remove your connection:** go to **Settings → My Connections**. You'll see **Connected**, **Expires soon**, **Needs reconnect** or **Not connected**, plus the renewal date. **Remove connection** disconnects WSO2 from your LinkedIn.

### 3. Top three amplifiers this month

A podium with the company's top three amplifiers for the current month. Names link to a person page.

Under the podium, **How monthly winners are chosen** spells out the rules:

- **100 points minimum** in the month to qualify.
- The **top three** advocates are recognized each month.
- If more than three tie for the top spots, WSO2 breaks the tie by post reach.

The board is company wide, not just your team. Totals refresh about every two hours, so a new share may take a little while to show.

### My position this month

Under the rule you'll see your **Rank**, your **Points**, and a one-line nudge, like:

- "Amplify a post this month to get on the board." (no points yet)
- "62 pts to qualify for the top 3."
- "You're in the top 3. Keep it up!"
- "40 pts to the top 3." or "40 pts to the next rank."

### 4. Team leaderboard

Your team's ranking, with rank, team member, house and points. You're highlighted with a **You** chip. Click **View full team** to open the whole roster, which also lists people who haven't earned points yet. You can search by name or email and sort by company rank, points or name.

If your team can't be shown, you'll see a short message instead:

| Message | What it means |
|---|---|
| "We couldn't find your team." | The employee directory has no team on record for you. Tell the Amplify team. |
| "Only you are shown: your team's roster isn't available right now." | The directory is down. Your row still counts. Try again later. |

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Amplify isn't connected yet" | The app isn't pointed at the Amplify service. Tell the Amplify team. |
| "Couldn't load the posts to amplify" (or top amplifiers, or team leaderboard) | Click **Retry**. If it keeps failing, tell the Amplify team. |
| A post you expect isn't in the list | Either you've already shared it, or it's no longer open. Posts are open for a limited window. |
| You shared a post but your points haven't moved | The share goes out with the next Amplify run, and the top-three board refreshes about every two hours. Check again later. |
| Pop-up keeps asking you to link | You chose "I don't consent" earlier. That's expected: it asks again on each Amplify click. |

## Quick reference

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Repost, no added text | 2 |
| Repost with your own thoughts | 5 |

Points are in whole numbers, earned once per post.
